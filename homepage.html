Why is the homepage free?

It is our first step, not a trick. We design one homepage concept from your brand so you can judge the work before paying anything. If you like it, we build the rest. If not, the concept is still yours to keep.

How fast can you deliver?

The free homepage concept arrives within 24 hours. A full website takes 2–3 weeks; an app takes 3–4 weeks. The timeline is agreed in writing before we start.

Do you use templates?

No. Every layout starts from your brand, your content and your customer. This page was designed from scratch for CodeCare — not assembled from a theme.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. We hand over the code and a short walkthrough. If you would rather not touch it, our Web Handle plan covers updates and small changes for you.

Do you do SEO?

Every build includes clean semantic HTML, metadata, fast loading and schema markup. Ongoing content and keyword campaigns can be added as a separate scope, priced upfront.

What happens after launch?

You can take everything and run it yourself, or hand it to us. Monthly handling covers hosting, backups, security, updates and fixes, with 24/7 support.

Do I own the website?

Yes — the code, domain, hosting accounts and keys are yours. We build on your accounts wherever possible, so there is never any lock-in.

How does pricing work?

Every project is quoted before we start, and that number is fixed once you agree to it. Websites, apps and monthly handling are priced separately, so you only pay for what you actually need — no hourly surprises.
